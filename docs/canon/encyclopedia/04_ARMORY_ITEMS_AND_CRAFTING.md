## Safe economic transactions and acquisition canon

- Workshop recipes check valid locations, professional skill, required finite ingredients, an available workstation, output space, and atomic save success.
- Crafting cannot create item rewards from prose, duplicate limited supplies, consume invalid inventory or force a player into an unrecoverable wage/consent dead end.
- Actual reward grants, inventory switches, vendor stock and unique relic migrations are governed by C++; the ledger identifies possible versus implemented means of acquisition.
- Named special items need a provenance trail, including observation or mastery where appropriate. The Atlas of Absence must not be treated as automatically granted simply for visiting a realm.
