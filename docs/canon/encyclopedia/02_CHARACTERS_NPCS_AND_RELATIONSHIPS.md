A literary character dossier does **not** establish that voice, animation, scenes or gameplay dialogue have been imported into Unreal.

**Registry reconciliation:** All 88 stable NPC identities in Remembered Roads have dedicated resident-return records. ## The playable traveler

A wandering outcast from an impossible history. Character identity is selected in play. Do not canonize one name, appearance, gender, family lineage, ending or compulsory moral alignment. The character can learn through physical study, but cannot copy a creature's abilities merely by encountering it. **Authority:** `UnmadeCharacterIdentityRules.h` and `UnmadeAbilityStudyRules.h`.
