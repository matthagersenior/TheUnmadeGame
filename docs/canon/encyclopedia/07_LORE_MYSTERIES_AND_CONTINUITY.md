# Volume VII — The Unanswered Interval, Literary Canon, Protected Mysteries and World Continuity

**Canon distinctions:** Volume X is a primary literary authority for future adaptations, characterization and motifs. Its new private motivations and unimplemented scenes are **literary/editorial canon**, not automatically UE-engine gameplay. An in-world report is not omniscient fact. Contradictory evidence can coexist; mysteries classified OPEN are intentionally unanswered.

## What is established

- The world is not fake: its impossible omissions have become materially real to communities living with them.
- The Unanswered Interval recurs as a missing bell note, erased sea, borrowed heat, future debt, absent choir, fossilized lineage, unentered citizens and the law of an uncrowned court.
- Nhal-Vey is an answering consciousness used to suppress questions; institutions and people remain accountable for their own decisions. Do not retroactively blame every injustice on a single monster.
- Consent, protected identity, firsthand evidence, rumor attribution, wages, local civic responsibility and saved actions are binding continuity constraints.
- The playable protagonist has no one canonized gender, origin, name or heirship. Held Morning and Many Mornings are *alternatives*, neither the mandatory universal literary history.
