# The Unmade — Cinematic Explainer and Nine-Realm Unreal Production Handoff
**10 October 2026 · Chapter companion to illustrated Volume IX**

## Delivered as source, not fake footage
A 149.5-second, sixteen-shot original-concept-art motion animatic was assembled from the 30-plate art atlas of illustrated Bible Volume VIII. Two H.264/AAC 960×540 versions exist as chat artifacts: temporary **offline synthetic English voiceover plus an original procedurally composed scratch score**, and music/caption-only. Every frame is marked **concept-art preproduction; not gameplay**, and the public film does NOT reveal Nhal-Vey's mask-breaking false victory, two postboss realities or the optional return rematch.

The **actual reusable build package** is the machine-readable `Authoring/cinematic_explainer_v1.json` (16 exact shot ids/durations, narration, art source, direction), `Authoring/cinematic_realm_playbook.json` (9 canon realms, physical verbs, named witnesses, mechanism, readable hazard, ethical resolutions, enemy, rite use, foreshadowing, postvictory variants, 4+ exact asset work IDs), and the standard-library-only `Scripts/build_cinematic_handoff.py`. Run `--write` after an authorized change and commit both generated Unreal CSVs; CI/first-PC preflight runs `--check` to reject content drift. The cinematic MP4 and original art are companion ZIP outputs, **not committed to GitHub** or represented as final engine scenes.

## Import guide in a supported Unreal Editor
1. Run `python Scripts/build_cinematic_handoff.py --check` and `python Scripts/build_world_content.py --check`. Do not open a project with broken authoring contracts. The JSON master must agree with the nine `Realm` enum IDs and exactly 16 serialized film shots; no extra runtime SaveGame fields were introduced.
2. Compile the game Editor target with UHT before touching DataTables. In Content Browser, create `DT_UnmadeCinematicShots` by importing `Authoring/generated/cinematic_shots_unreal.csv` as the `FUnmadeCinematicShotAuthoringRow` row struct, and `DT_UnmadeCinematicRealms` from `cinematic_realms_unreal.csv` as `FUnmadeCinematicRealmAuthoringRow`. Both row types live in `Source/TheUnmadeGame/Public/Authoring/UnmadeCinematicAuthoringRows.h`. **No actual Editor import has yet been performed**.
3. The `Name` column is a stable row key (`S01..S16`, `ThreefoldReach..FirstAbsence`). Do not key art or VO to mutable display names. Reimport existing assets when master JSON changes, rather than creating duplicate DataTables.
4. The CSVs are DataTable metadata, **not native Level Sequence keyframes**. Author sixteen shot Level Sequences or one master Sequence, create CineCameraActor objects and smooth framing/keyframes, localize subtitles keyed to IDs, then replace temp narration with 48 kHz WAV stems named `VO_S01.wav` ... `VO_S16.wav`. Record/edit the track under a creator-owned recording agreement. Preserve separate dialogue and music tracks for accessibility.
5. Each realm recipe includes at least three **physical** player verbs, rather than decorative cinematics. Build actual Blueprint/C++ world components that change collision and navigation after an earned choice, play a readable warning before hazards, validate evidence from real player interaction and persist mutually exclusive care/truth outcomes. Connect existing `UnmadePrototypeHub` tagged cubes/actors as the source smoke-test until your authored assets have real parity.
6. Final-boss presentation: preserve the actual native two required boss forms, directional telegraphed strike footprints, twice-confirmed world choice and optional third echo. Never replace authoritative saved `FinalJourney` with a cosmetic Sequencer completion signal. Both changed realities share the same existing save/world paths and have genuinely different local outcomes.
7. For the cinematic film, preserve the public no-spoiler policy. A separate private *developer ending reel* may show the mask fracture and postboss realities after player consent, but must not get silently included in the opening trailer or first-play experience.
8. Test all nine realm entries via source and then in PIE: actual camera and sound output, subtitles/high contrast, character/avatar consistency, controller equivalence, no forced inversion of input mapping, reliable saved-world continuity, skippable/pauseable cinematics and real packed Win64 playback.
9. Rebuild the animatic from the bundled `build_cinematic.py` and `render_animatic.py` **in the exported conversation project** with ffmpeg, PIL, numpy, scipy, soundfile and espeak installed, or replace the scratch audio entirely. The scripts intentionally use previous volume concept art as input and are not a standalone Unreal scene authoring command.

## Art and sound ownership / honesty
Do not pitch the cinematic as captured gameplay. The art is original fictional visual-development material from an earlier illustrated edition; some paintings were small atlas crops and should be replaced with rendered in-engine shots after PC acquisition. The procedural synthetic score and deliberately machine-like scratch voice do not represent the finished sound mix, VO casting or game characters' performed voices. The video is a **director's early proof of style and rhythm**, not an asset manifest for a commercial trailer.
