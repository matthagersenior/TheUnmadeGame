# THE UNMADE — Realm-scale physical adventure contract
**October 10, 2026 | Four player pillars | Source targets versus built content**

## The four-player promise
Freedom with lasting, understandable consequences. A living community that remembers only what it can know. Earned mastery without repeated chores. Exploration where detours reveal new paths, not empty marker counts. Local authored gameplay and saves must run without AI, internet, inference subscription or paid service.

## Existing production baseline
- **Bellwold / The Second Night:** evidence and distinct witness, then saved exclusive ward or census monument (graybox source; engine untested).
- **Saltwake / The Storm's Second Invoice:** after the original settlement story, physically operate Storm Sluice to disable flood barrier; visit Cistern + Thenn or Rain Ledger + Sella; commit care or public truth near Harrow. The result opens one downstream gate and forms a Safe Jetty or Open Tide Walk, changes local speech and remains in saved geometry. *Source pass; actual flood mechanics, inhabitants' livelihood simulation and world traversal unverified.*
- **Cinderhold / The Ember That Chose No Heir:** after the original hearth resolution, operate Heat Vent to remove heat seal; investigate Common Kiln + Ovenna or Ember Deed + Tarin; return to Rheva. A Public Kiln or Unsealed Passage, mutually exclusive route gates and remembered local consequences persist. *Source pass; simulated thermal damage, live fire, additional interior navigation and new combat are not present.*

## Non-negotiable implementation/release gate for *each* realm
1. Authored, navigable environment with clear hazard tell and two discoverable solutions; no fake background quest markers.
2. At least one physically operated world-state control and one meaningful counterplay action beyond pressing E; positive feedback and affordable retry.
3. Local named NPC routines, reactions to witnessed and rumored events, optional contextual dialogue; no magical memory.
4. Firsthand evidence, named witness, previewed nontrivial consequence and irreversible confirm; both resolutions materially alter usable geometry, access or economy.
5. Reward and discovery designed to reward attention and curiosity once, never an endlessly repeating chore.
6. Atomic save/restore, corrupt data rejection, repeated-load robustness, player-facing journal, controller accessibility and explicit no-LLM dependency.
7. Offline C++ tests, Python wiring checks, Unreal Editor build, test map launch, manual traversal of two endings and an end-to-end creator playtest on a supported host.
8. Canon and illustrated package DOCX/PDF/art atlas/source appendix/manifest/ZIP must move together in one dated edition. Drawn concepts must be labeled as speculative.

Only after these eight checks may a later realm move from **atlas contract** to **verified playable destination**. A compiled graybox still is not a production-ready open-world continent.
