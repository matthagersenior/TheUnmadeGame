# Player experience integration v17 acceptance

All seven approved upgrade areas are source-implemented. This is a source and portable-content milestone. Unreal compilation, imports, native UI rendering and engine playtesting remain deferred by the creator. Existing campaign outcomes, all ten disciplines, stable item/actor identities, wages, consent and nine OPEN mysteries retain authority.

## Combat and unified loadout

The equipment component owns independent realm weapon, armor and passive technique selections. Absent new fields, old expedition equipment is migrated in memory; a new optional three-value loadout validates actual earned rewards before saving. Cycling one slot preserves the other, replaces that slot's native item bonuses, and retains charm modifiers. Current inventory labels, slot comparisons, combat style and reality discounts share this authority. Canonical item IDs and the legacy inventory remain intact. A claimed reward is durable even if a subsequent equip write fails; it can be selected later.

A melee attack spends stamina and reserves a monotonic swing at windup, then performs capsule/pawn-object blade-span and arc sweeps during a bounded contact window. Cover LOS and facing exclude obstructed targets; each reserved swing can claim one target, even if a notify repeats. Imported WeaponTip sockets may drive the endpoint; the fallback is a timed spatial arc. Animation rate follows weapon rhythm. A native UAnimNotify and guarded Editor install script provide three contact events for the imported Resident Attack clip; the script is dry-run unless --execute is supplied in a real Editor with compiled classes and imported assets. No installed notifies or animation graph is claimed.

Expedition marksmen and ordinary Watchers now fire finite, non-homing swept shots toward locked warning aims. The legacy redacted focus also launches a shot at its contact event. Pawn and static/dynamic world collisions stop shots, including cover; dodge and parry use the same receipt rules. Actual player shot kills reach existing earned-reward handling. Directional melee injury feedback distinguishes front/back/left/right. Stagger resistance prevents repeated uninterrupted stuns, with longer resistance for bosses. Original boss footprints and legal evidence resolutions remain. Traveling projectiles are prototype spheres, not final effects. Low-frame-rate contact sampling, sockets, animation phase, cover interception and readability require engine acceptance.

## Nine optional wings

Each existing six-room dungeon keeps its saved logical IDs and gains a three-room authored wing: 27 additional room areas, nine real named guardians, and earned physical return shortcuts. Source/atlas coordinates come from one authored registry. Main story prerequisites and the old optional v15/v16 routes remain unchanged. All wings are optional; their rooms are graybox, not finished levels.

Crossings uses a real consenting field worker who follows the nearby player's collision-safe breadcrumbs, waiting when the player leaves them. Saltwake requires low-water stations and has a bounded later surge with a dry edge route. Cinderhold relays reset after a missed twenty-second interval. Drevlach requires guarded oath stones and permits the actual wounded duelist to yield alive below one-quarter health. Orravane requires elevated ledges; Vathless returns to a paired record at low strain; Eillun requires crouched unobserved readings around a real rotating/patrolling Index Lamp Sentry whose actual defeat persists; Tharniv requires an earned Living Roads chapter; Auvren includes a moving lifted platform above a safe lower floor. These are logic and primitive traversal implementations; they are not full fluid, plant, spatial-rewrite or physics simulations.

The source guardian resolution validates the real actor, its encounter identity and actual defeat, then commits before granting insight. Yield requires the living actor and exact physical conditions. Failed saves retry without inventing a victory. Guardians use existing realm signature patterns with reduced health; they are nine authored identities, not nine wholly new AI architectures.

## Characters, settlements and consequences

The existing 45 expedition residents gain day-bounded recognition and 36 conditional authored scenes: fieldworker introduction, guardian witness, disputed accord, and rescued people's return. Each scene requires its real speaker nearby and commits once before blocking/gesture/caption. Captions retain priority for a reading interval based on their length. New reactions append to earlier personal conversations rather than replacing them. Direct local reserve/witness/repair/mediation events enter existing perception-limited social memory; no remote global reputation is invented.

All 29 settlements receive furnished primitive service rooms with counters, a work table, bed, hearing/reading area, shared-room gate and repair rubble. Named specialties differ across nine realms. Normal rooms open 06:00–22:00; Cinderhold uses continuous shifts; Eillun works 18:00–06:00. Existing provisioners/crafting/trainers honor these hours. Region stock remains shared and finite. Privileges permit two specialty uses, once per game day, with real strain recovery, heat pressure, brief evasion, status clearing, quiet lens, healing, warding, bearings or mediation according to realm. These are limited local services, not a full merchant inventory/economic simulation.

Repairs clear actual rubble and reopen the work table; existing field workers and rescued residents route to work. Public witness brings the existing return-road claimant and a public hearing; protected reserve opens beds. A repaired table plus a second-day real witness meeting can mediate shared access without erasing the accord, claimant record, wages or disagreement. Primitive hall-entry waypoints route residents around the room walls when navmesh is missing; steering remains collision-swept. Engine navmesh and furnishing clearance still require acceptance. These routines do not create new wage payments or a salaried job simulation.

## Progression and interface

Wing resolution grants five insight; four conditional relationship scenes can add four, with exact conservation. Duelist, Warden and Pathfinder builds have bounded attack/armor, stamina, speed and reach tradeoffs. Build selection has its own saved authority and can freely re-equip an owned realm build. First build costs two insight, changing its type costs one; a tool, crafted binding, privilege or repair costs one. Tools open physical wing shortcuts. Crafted bindings add two armor and extend field-kit suppression from twenty to thirty seconds. Finite spending is explicit; it does not modify marks, wages, personal favor or legacy crafting.

The optional 231-integer experience snapshot validates scene, progression, purchase, day, discovery and spending dependencies against restored expedition/depth state. Old saves omit it. Invalid snapshots block world writes; failed changes restore state, relay sessions and clock before rewards. Settings live in their own user config. Partial combat, relay timers, breadcrumbs and recent captions are transient.

The native interface supplies an objective tracker, five journal categories, per-page action groups, earned city/room/wing markers and recorded bearings. Arriving in a city persists discovery; unknown geometry and unoffered/unheard personal options remain hidden. Map selection never teleports or grants quests. Equipment comparisons display slot deltas and distinct passive/active techniques. Text scaling, camera sensitivity, sound volume, high contrast, reduced flashes, tutorial visibility and prompt mode are configurable. Attack/guard/dodge/interact/technique can be rebound independently for keyboard/mouse and controller; reserved/conflicting keys are rejected. Menu UI-only input, controller page navigation and paired pause/input restoration exclude gameplay while open. Old story subtitles and trackers remain.

Six-hour resting requires actual shared-room access and a second confirmation. World time advances atomically and existing obligations remain due. Defeat recovery now selects the nearest earned city safe platform while retaining story, inventory and memories. No punitive wage confiscation, forced ending or additional death economy is introduced.
