# Adventure depth v16 implementation and acceptance

This source milestone implements the six approved improvement areas over the existing nine-realm game. It preserves prior campaign decisions, ten signature disciplines, wages, consent, stable item IDs, 133 registered residents, 29 settlements and nine OPEN mysteries. Unreal compilation and playtesting remain deferred. Source implementation does not establish shipping quality.

## Exploration

Nine distinct six-chamber grid paths replace the shared switchback. Each path has consecutive physical doors, a gate on its actual boss entry and a northern earned return exit. Connected floors and an outer return walkway are rebuilt from each layout's bounds. Existing evidence, guards, captives, boss and inspection points follow those room coordinates; v15 valves move with chamber three. The stable expedition snapshot retains completed logical chamber IDs, so old saves remain compatible with the new geometry.

Additional optional route challenges are distinct: Crossings bell sequence 1,3,2; Saltwake pump mask 1 and 3 during seconds 4 through 9 of a twelve-second cycle; Cinderhold three cooling relays within eighteen seconds; Drevlach weight controls 1 and 2 totaling five; Orravane three anchors on a physical upper walkway/ramp; Vathless memory shutter sequence 1,2,3; Eillun three lamp readings while unseen or after clearing the patrol; Tharniv root sequence 3,1,2; Auvren boundary mirrors 2 and 3. Wrong attempts remain retryable. Completion opens a physical side-alcove gate and grants two local favor. Hidden named caches require one crafted field kit, registered chamber four and the open route, then grant two favor and access to the final mastery lesson. The challenge session is transient; only completed routes/caches persist.

These are authored graybox mechanisms. They are not finished flooded scenery, animated root growth, a moving-room simulation or final environmental art. Main dungeon paths remain accessible without optional challenges. Original v15 valve puzzles and survey rewards remain supporting content.

## Combat

Every expedition weapon now has a native style with distinct reach (235–420 cm), strike rhythm (0.45–1.15 seconds), stamina cost (11–24), damage multiplier and finishing status. Existing heavy blades, the long needle, tempered blade and redacted focus also gain styles. Other legacy weapons retain a balanced style. Previously owned expedition weapon bonuses remain authoritative; that active weapon takes precedence over a legacy weapon's style until the player equips another expedition kit.

Player stamina starts at 100, regenerates at 14 per second plus two per mastery level, and drains at nine per second while guarding. Three strikes inside the combo window produce a stronger finisher, brief stagger and Slow/Burn/Weaken/Expose. Dodge spends 22 stamina and uses collision-aware CharacterMovement launch, with a short evasion window improved by mastery. Guard entry gives a timed parry window; successful parries cost five stamina and stagger the attacker. Repeated guard entry has a recovery interval. Defended hits and hazard pulses receive duplicate-proof receipts. Revival retains monotonically increasing outgoing swing IDs. Burn pulses are identified and bounded; statuses expire and do not persist through a session restart.

Enemy groups vary by realm, maintain the existing flanker/marksman/disruptor behaviors, offer four armor from a nearby actively guarding sentinel, and permit at most two ordinary expedition attack warnings concurrently. Crouching reduces long-distance detection; actual sight and facing determine whether a stealth control is observed. Existing bosses retain their v15 signature footprints. Locomotion respects Slow and attack patterns respect stagger. This is deterministic local combat, not an animation-driven hitbox or projectile system.

## Personal stories and consequences

Nine named local stories connect the frontier leader and refuge/road-watch witness already in the roster. Each requires an offered quest after frontier arrival, a real witness interaction, physical evidence in chamber four, and a resolved expedition boss before its final accord. Explicit paired choices have distinct authored offers, witness testimony, evidence, options and return conversations. The same choice must be confirmed again beside the real leader within twenty game seconds; opening a paused menu suspends that game-time interval. An opposite choice replaces the pending preview, never changes an already committed accord.

A protected reserve earns four local favor and cheaper provisions; public witness earns three favor and cheaper training. Prices and dialogue express those local allegiances without overwriting the older social-memory model. These are local accords, not forced canonical endings. No mystery origin is revealed by a threshold diagram, ancestor record or census page.

## Settlements and progression

Provisioner, field-kit crafting and mastery stations are created in all 29 existing settlements: eleven original cities and eighteen frontier/refuge sites. Their finite stock and favor balances are shared by realm. Provisioners have four purchases total per realm, at most one per game day, and refuse payment at full health. Crafting costs two favor and makes a field kit up to capacity three. A kit opens one cache or temporarily grounds an unresolved local danger field for twenty seconds. Already quiet, resolved or surveyed fields do not consume a kit.

Three mastery levels improve stamina recovery, parry and dodge. The second lesson requires the route; the third requires the cache. Finite favor supports meaningful spending choices. All levels are reachable on either personal-quest branch with a crafted cache kit. Selected earned techniques use F11, cost thirty stamina and have twelve-second recovery: exposed openings, slowing pressure, burning heat, weakening oath, interruption, seam stagger/exposure, evasive step, guarded healing, and a six-second damage ward. These extend equipment techniques; they do not replace the original ten disciplines. Legacy crafting, inns/sanctuary, stock, marks and regional professions remain available; this pass does not create full inn interiors or a new career system.

The optional versioned 110-integer depth snapshot validates dependencies against expedition progress and current day, bounded resources, spending, training and active selection. Old saves omit it. Corrupt data blocks world writes; failed writes roll back permanent state and transient challenge state before healing, inventory feedback or physical changes. Stamina, temporary status and partial puzzle progress are intentionally transient.

## Player interface and assets

A native UMG interface constructs itself before its Slate widget is rebuilt. It retries creation after pawn possession. Health, stamina, strain, visible-target health/warnings and context prompts are shown. Journal, equipment and selectable nine-realm map panels scroll, show prior campaign/witness/household/economy information and expose equipment/consumable controls and nearby personal-choice buttons. Map selection supplies bearings and room coordinates, never teleports. Dialogues subscribe to the existing authored/optional-local-model event. A wrapped action row, readable text, keyboard focus, Escape closure and paired input restoration are supplied. The local world pauses while menus are open and resumes only if this widget paused it. The existing authored cinematic subtitle widgets remain in use. All existing gameplay on-screen notices now route through the active local RPG interface and its bounded recent-event history; early initialization and missing local viewports retain a diagnostic fallback; a complete shipping accessibility pass is still required.

Three original rigs now carry 19 clips each, 57 total, adding Dodge and Parry. Offline pose previews include walking extrema as well as middle/end poses. Prior sounds and illustrations are retained. These are original low-poly prototype animation sources, not motion capture, a completed animation graph or imported assets.

## Engine acceptance required

Compile the full Unreal project/UHT; import all 57 clips and retained sounds. Test UI creation after possession and rebuild, 720p/1080p/ultrawide/DPI fit, keyboard/gamepad focus, scroll retention, tabs, combat exclusion while menus are open, pause restoration and cinematic transitions. Inspect native GUI rather than relying on the static source checks. Exercise every room route, actual gate/floor seams, ramps, height checks, LOS, alcove collision, nav rebuild and return walkway. Test all quest branches, choice previews/expiry, reload, write failure and malformed snapshots; prove every mastery branch reachable with actual spending and no stock reset between settlements. Test guard/parry timing, evasion receipt deduplication, status expiry, burn kills, team-warning limits, stamina exhaustion and revive receipts. Tune actual animation/sound timing, combat feel, enemy fairness and performance in PIE. Portable tests and source contracts do not replace these checks.

Public-witness accords additionally create nine conditional named return-road claimants, one per realm. Actual combat defeat commits a non-respawning outcome and two local favor; failed writes retry without inventing victory. Protected-reserve accords avoid this encounter. New total content adds nine conditional opponents to the existing expedition roster. This does not select a campaign ending.
