# THE UNMADE — THE TENFOLD CHRONICLES
## Original quests, reality disciplines, consequence design and extended campaign
**Story systems implemented as deterministic C++ source, October 9 2026. In-engine play and full visual experience not yet verified.**

The governing fantasy: **knowledge, memory, responsibility and the laws of existence are playable resources**. Powers cost Reality Strain, leave traces that actual witnesses can remember, and create material consequences. The player can be a witness, protector, archivist, fighter, smith, cartographer and imperfect oathkeeper without depending on LLMs, procedural filler or mandatory online content.

## How the tenfold quest progression works

Every discipline has five distinct, saveable chapters:

1. **Discovery:** Physically find its authored inscription, after the necessary existing exploration/quest accomplishments.
2. **Testimony:** Speak to the correct named resident within sight and interaction range; a distant or invented conversation does not count.
3. **Trial:** Actually activate the new mechanic near its inscription, paying finite Strain and observing a cooldown or other risk.
4. **Consequence:** Obtain at least two valid firsthand witnessing NPCs near the scene, then make a persistent **protect** or **reveal** decision.
5. **Mastery:** Return to its inscription with additional accomplishments uniquely relevant to that discipline. A mastered rite awards exactly one original relic.

The first two levels of a rite are **learning**, not unlimited access to the strongest version. Different quests require visiting multiple settlements, understanding Veyl, helping displaced citizens, winning or resolving a boss encounter, earning trust, surviving a deferred debt, or proving three *different* deeds. Completion cannot be farmed by repeating a nearby NPC line. Six disciplines accrue separate mastery records: Unmaker, Witness, Elseborn, Relicwright, Oathkeeper, and Pathweaver.

### 1. Unwrite a Law — The Law Beneath the Last Bell
**Region:** Bellgrave, Threefold Reach. **NPC:** the bell maker. **Lore:** A forgotten decree keeps gravity, sound and momentum obedient. The buried bell contains a syllable cut out of it.
**Playable trial:** Choose gravity, sound or momentum. Gravity launches the player into otherwise hard-to-reach space; sound staggers nearby threats; momentum pushes nearby enemies. A bounded area and cooldown prevent spam.
**Decision:** Prevent suffering through silence, or reveal the decree at a cost. Protection eventually slightly lowers ability Strain cost; revealing extends its duration.
**Mastery:** Face a real boss before safely binding the bell's law. **Reward:** the Bellmaker's Unwritten Verse.
**Quest twist:** A town needs an alarm even when sound itself is dangerous; disable the wrong warning at the wrong moment and people may distrust the choice.

### 2. Witnesscraft — The Bridge of Living Testimony
**Region:** Well of Returned Voices. **NPC:** well listener. **Lore:** The first bridges were promises. When the last direct witnesses died, their crossing stopped existing.
**Playable trial:** Find at least two people who personally observed a relevant event, then make an unstable missing bridge physically manifest. Rumors alone are insufficient.
**Decision:** Stabilize the crossing for refugees or preserve its proof for scholars. The choice becomes firsthand or rumor-backed memory in individual NPCs.
**Mastery:** Earn three reliable witnesses; the prototype may stabilize the authored bridge beyond its normal timed appearance.
**Reward:** The Bridge That Remembers.
**Quest twist:** The easiest testimony is not necessarily true. Evidence provenance matters.

### 3. Borrowed Lives — The Life Not Taken
**Region:** Orchard of Unwritten Names. **NPC:** displaced orchard keeper. **Lore:** One leaf contains the player's signature beneath the title of a profession they never learned.
**Playable trial:** For a finite interval choose an alternate self: forgotten warrior (attack), unborn artisan (advanced crafting skill without free materials), or unwritten archivist (defense and different authored NPC recognition). Players can switch the *selection* at a ritual stone but cannot keep all lives active at once.
**Decision:** Reveal or conceal the identity from witnesses. **Mastery:** Carry a possible self's story beyond the first realm.
**Reward:** The Second Name.
**Quest twist:** An NPC may recognize the borrowed profession even when they do not recognize the hero.

### 4. Legacy Forging — A Blade That Was There
**Region:** Bellwold forge. **NPC:** stonewright. **Lore:** Steel refuses to carry a name unless it has personally protected, discovered or reconciled.
**Playable trial:** Temporarily awaken a weapon, gaining strength from different deeds. Record three distinct categories earned through actual helping, exploration and peacemaking, **not three repeated kills**.
**Decision:** Make the blade an archive of survival or a public confession.
**Mastery:** Earn all three distinct deed histories. **Reward:** The Three-Deed Temper.
**Quest twist:** Two heroes can wield the same blade type but have different stories written into its edges. A full branching mesh/evolution interface is future art/gameplay work.

### 5. Living Roads — No Road Belongs to One
**Region:** Silent Mile. **NPC:** road warden. **Lore:** The road once connected every community that agreed to meet; property claims severed its original destination.
**Playable trial:** Form a short-lived physical causeway. Travel and testimony establish more than simply pressing a map pin.
**Decision:** Hold open the crossing for residents or privilege a verified official route.
**Mastery:** Visit all three settlements and resolve both Bellwold and Paperhaven's faction disputes. **Reward:** The Common Causeway.
**Quest twist:** Diplomacy becomes an infrastructure mechanic and can permanently stabilize a section of route.

### 6. Tomorrow's Debt — The Ledger of Borrowed Dawn
**Region:** Debt Market. **NPC:** keeper of future debts. **Lore:** The ledger contains the player's receipt dated tomorrow. No lender is permitted to hand their fatigue to an innocent.
**Playable trial:** Gain a powerful short-term attack bonus but register an unavoidable due day. A second loan is prohibited until repayment, followed by a limited period of weakened attack.
**Decision:** Borrow for refuge or to expose corruption. **Mastery:** Actually survive the debt's due day; earning a permanent reward never makes the debt disappear.
**Reward:** Tomorrow's Paid Receipt.
**Quest twist:** The temptation is real, but the bill always comes due—even after a victory.

### 7. Understand a Boss — The Name Beneath the Monster
**Region:** Bellwold ossuary. **NPC:** refuge matron. **Lore:** The Hollow Keeper was condemned under a name that the archive erased. Violence perpetuates the injury.
**Playable trial:** Gather firsthand observers and approach the Hollow Bell encounter, then invoke a memory strong enough to end the fight nonlethally. Existing physical combat remains an alternative.
**Decision:** Preserve the keeper's human testimony or reveal the institution that buried it.
**Mastery:** Resolve the boss event through the real encounter, not an NPC's invented claim. **Reward:** Mercy of the Hollow Keeper.
**Quest twist:** Mercy must be researched and risked. The boss is not instantly pacified by a generic dialogue option; the player needs real evidence and proximity. Its memory can later be summoned in the paired-history confluence.

### 8. Paradox Convergence — The Tower That Fell and Stood
**Region:** Paperhaven scriptorium. **NPC:** mapmaker. **Lore:** The archive tower fell in a century the people deny happened, while its intact rooms still hold residents in another history.
**Playable trial:** Temporarily show an alternate physical tower while the ruined history remains legible.
**Decision:** Preserve the missing ruin as history, or allow the intact and ruined structure to remain superimposed.
**Mastery:** Carry credible Veyl and faction testimony between histories.
**Reward:** The Room Between Histories.
**Quest twist:** The two histories can coexist, and the chosen form persists in the world after a completed save.

### 9. Oathbinding — The Lantern You Promised
**Region:** Bellwold Refuge. **NPC:** watch captain. **Lore:** Protective oaths belong to the vulnerable, not to rulers, and are stronger precisely because they can be broken.
**Playable trial:** Bind temporary defense to a witnessed shelter promise, or choose a truth-oriented battle stance after mastery. The player may consciously break their oath, recorded by individual NPC witnesses.
**Decision:** Honor shelter or disclose a dangerous truth.
**Mastery:** Honor the vow and complete Bellwold's earlier relief task.
**Reward:** The Unbroken Shelter Oath.
**Quest twist:** Betrayal costs trust and the ability, but does not permanently soft-lock the full story. A difficult redemption requires **three distinct deeds, a resolved Bellwold faction arc and three eyewitnesses**; people remember both betrayal and restitution.

### 10. The Unreliable Map — The Map of Disagreeing Shores
**Region:** Saltwake harbor, The Rain That Forgot the Sea. **NPC:** drowned navigator. **Lore:** Nobody agrees where the ocean ended. Maps claiming certainty have sent families to their deaths.
**Playable trial:** Draw a provisional route, listen to firsthand navigation history, and physically test a frontier crossing. A rumor cannot automatically become a trusted shortcut.
**Decision:** Publish the disputed route or annotate it honestly.
**Mastery:** Verify at least one route through real exploration after the navigator's testimony. **Reward:** Atlas of the Missing Coast.
**Quest twist:** A false shortcut is more dangerous than no shortcut. The map tracks source confidence, not merely discovery.

## Six Confluences — mastery is a beginning, not an ending

These are separately saved, physically placed challenges. Each has three progression stages: find its inscribed chamber **after mastering both specified disciplines**, invoke the two different powers in the **same place within 120 in-world seconds**, then settle the outcome with at least two credible witnesses. The final chamber requires all ten rites and all five prior confluences, plus frontier travel.

| Challenge | Combined powers | Stakes | Mythic reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Alarm Nobody Heard | Unwrite + Witnesscraft | Silence a lethal toll without erasing the town's warning | Aegis of the Last Warning |
| A Blade for Both Your Names | Borrowed Lives + Legacy Forging | Decide which self's deeds the metal remembers | Edge of the Elseborn Smith |
| The Route Nobody Owned | Living Roads + Cartography | Fix a route without inventing its provenance | Chart of the Common Path |
| Tomorrow Is Not Yours to Spend | Tomorrow's Debt + Oathbinding | Protect a refuge without forcing someone else to repay | Heart of the Borrowed Dawn |
| The Bell Fell and Stood | Boss Understanding + Paradox | Remember both keeper and creature | Mirror of the Two Keepers |
| The Place Before a First Story | Unwrite + Cartography, after all ten | A first-realm capstone about whether a way home can belong to everyone | Witness of the First Absence |

The last confluence does **not** end THE UNMADE. It resolves the first large signature-ability saga and sets up the later realms; six of the atlas's nine realms remain only designs. An eventual full RPG conclusion is a separate, larger narrative and art project.

## How the campaign stays substantial without filler

1. **Earned challenges:** Different disciplines are gated by real travel, quests, historical evidence, personal witness memory, saved faction outcomes and boss confrontation. Merely unlocking an ability is not mastery.
2. **Distinct verbs and risks:** Fight, spare, testify, verify, build, borrow, repay, forge, protect, reveal, map, make alternate identities and stabilize matter. The implementation has initial distinct physical/technical effects, but the larger authored encounters still need level design.
3. **No forced repetitive grind:** No infinite weak-enemy farming, loot boxes, mandatory online AI calls, or arbitrary daily-login chores. Repeatable practice may exist, but one-time meaningful challenges award rare relics.
4. **Failure without trapping the player:** Reality Strain and cooldowns matter, witnesses must be gathered, a broken oath has a costly redemption path, and a borrowed future strength leads to real temporary weakness.
5. **Replayability from consequence:** Protection versus disclosure changes NPC trust, certain power costs or durations, and some world variants. Full branching cutscenes, settlements, economies and faction war simulation remain future Unreal content, not done features.
6. **Difficulty should come from learning systems:** Boss telegraphs, timed two-power puzzles, balancing evidence, and weighing costs. Only real playtesting can establish the eventual campaign length, difficulty curves, accessibility, and whether missions stay fresh.

## Developer integrity / content boundary

- **In source:** all ten deterministic rite rules, authored chapter text, abilities with bounded costs and cooldowns, 10 ritual stones, their E/F-key input hooks, named NPC dialogue hook, eyewitness provenance, 58 authored items, 6 confluence chambers, saved one-time rewards, debt exhaustion, oath/redemption, and a combined offline-native C++ test.
- **Partially in source:** physical temporary bridges/towers, hero physics launch, enemy staggering, push impulses, nonlethal Hollow Keeper outcome, simple profession borrowing, time-based buffs, witness/rumor interpretation and persistent relationship effects. Unreal execution has not been proved.
- **Not yet completed:** an actual Unreal build, bespoke 3D environments, animated creatures and combat choreography, proper sound-based silence propagation, detailed alternate-life dialogue branches, working UMG quest/inventory/map interfaces, full settlement restoration, weather, deep item meshes/evolution, world partition and real-time frame profiling.
- **No definitive playtime claim:** more steps and lore in source do not themselves prove a long, entertaining shipped campaign. We need developer playtests, authored mission layouts, skill-balance passes, player feedback and optimization before making that claim.
