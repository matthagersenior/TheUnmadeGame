# The Unmade — Six Quiet Observatories: Cross-Realm Abilities, Combat Memory and Witnessed Discovery
**Canon source addendum — 10 October 2026, source-real, UE-playtest pending.**

The ten learned disciplines are no longer merely independent hotkeys or a fixed confluence reward list. Six **optional, remote observations** now ask the player to bring two meaningfully different mastered powers to the same visible physical site. The site exists only after the corresponding third-chapter Echo decision, ensuring that the power amplifies a lived community consequence rather than replacing or invalidating it. This is deliberate fairness: players who do not complete these masteries can still travel and finish each realm's major narrative chapters. The reward is a permanent bridge to a story archive and dialogue recognition, **not a randomly farmed weapon or power stat**.

In the rule model, a successful ability cast records the paired RiteId against the persistent world clock. An unrelated or unmastered ability is refused. Two casts of the same ability never count as the required second discipline. The complementary mastered ability must be cast within 120 world-seconds, in either order. Once opened, the observatory stays open forever in that save; a second visit reads a durable record without changing inventory. A failed save restores the old optional state, rather than silently unlocking the path. The original cast still pays its normal cost and obeys cooldowns.

## Six authored archive events

### Drevlach — The Calendar Without a Creditor
*Tomorrow Debt + Oathbinding*. Veynu of the Ninth Due could choose to keep the child hidden or make the fraud public, but a new question follows: can an oath safeguard a future without binding children to a lender's contracts? The archive preserves calendar entries for borrowers who **refused** a debt, proving their absences as thoroughly as an ordinary signature. Orshel Drywake can point to the resulting ferry rules from direct experience; Ezzun Aftermark can dispute how the dates were written. Neither suddenly knows what the other witnessed.

### Orravane — The Gravity of the Unheard
*Unwrite Law + Paradox Convergence*. Yllesh Underchord's support choir has two real, incompatible arrangements. The archive holds both simultaneously without erasing the original singers. A player who opened the shared meter sees how homes remained stable; one who exposed the open stage hears which names were stripped from the harmony. Vhaurn Counterweight still values structural survival, while Orru of the Removed Rest insists on voluntary participation. The powers are a physical, timed proof, not a command to silence one NPC forever.

### Vathless — The Ancestor Under the Anvil
*Legacy Forging + Understanding Bosses*. A quarry guardian's name can be read beneath the scars on a deed-tempered blade. Ghraet Last-Seam and Torrun-Mortar must confront the difference between a stone protected by a person and a person reduced to building material. Issil Againborn may believe the family account without proving every chronology. The archive records worked stone provenance, whether the player chose Hearth Brace or Ancestor Wall. **Mastered understanding** is required; players cannot erase or instantly pacify arbitrary enemies by guessing a hidden boss name.

### Eillun — The Witness Without a Birth
*Witnesscraft + Borrowed Lives*. Nhel of No-Entry discovers a testimony that is valid without an official birth; a borrowed life may suggest an alternative craft but must never overwrite a resident's consent. Auvet Keep-Quiet keeps people's names private; Veysha Never-Signed insists on voluntary standing. The archive records only authorized statements. Neither successful rite gives the player permission to interrogate an unwilling citizen or fabricate an origin.

### Tharniv — The Path the Crown Refused
*Living Roads + Oathbinding*. Serriv Crownless understands that an unowned path can become another disguised throne if someone controls access. Imro Root-Vote argues for daily shared harvest; Eliun No-Coronation warns that a court of disputed claims can still impose coercive law. The paired masteries establish a real bridge to a public arboretum record where access promises and dissent sit side by side.

### Auvren — The Map That Kept Both Doors
*Unwrite Law + Cartography*. Orriv Before-Arrival and Veilune Both-Mornings remember incompatible truths. Thae of the Near Door protects ordinary travelers who need a route out before debating the universe. The observatory keeps both maps and one explicitly marked return path; it does **not** declare a universal beginning or erase earlier realm endings. The archive can be visited again after additional travels.

## Practical Unreal build contract
`RealmResonances` records six stable `Realm` and two `RiteId` pairs, including all ten existing rites at least once. `AUnmadePrototypeHub` spawns a plinth at the northern upper terrace, a separate bridge, a new island and an inspectable archive, with state reconstructed from the new optional schema-1 `UUnmadePrototypeSave` arrays. `UUnmadeTenfoldComponent::InvokeRite` reports a **mastered, successful, persisted cast** to the hub. Source-level NPC dialogue references local opened archives without rewriting the separate witness-memory/rumor system. New native and Unreal-automation source tests check the pairings, time and state contracts, save rejection and geometrical reachability.

The scene is not final: prototype cubes are not final observatory architecture, Unreal Engine has not compiled this change, full combat interactions still need authored enemy encounters and animations, and dialogue is presently textual. The next milestone should implement learnable combat foes whose behaviors respond to actual mastered abilities without invalidating ethical alternatives.
