# Remembered Roads source and engine acceptance

Source coverage: nine canonical realms, eleven settlements, all 82 registered residents and six existing field scouts. Unreal compilation, imports and PIE intentionally deferred by the creator. This document describes the implemented source and the future engine acceptance pass; it does not claim that pass ran.

## Complete walkthrough

1. Use existing earned travel to reach any settlement. Find its named sender (registry `Authoring/remembered_roads_v13.json`). Speak within 260 cm and clear sight. Cinderhold requires the completed Blackslag return and inherits that saved route; the other ten cities choose a new local route.
2. Go to the physical loading yard at city center plus `(3800,900)`. Read the three records at offsets `(-150,300)`, `(-150,650)`, `(-150,1000)`. E is free; F costs eight Strain and does not reveal remote knowledge.
3. The mechanism lies at yard plus `(300,1000)`. With all records read, E opens a 12-second quiet window; E again quiets the actual living elder. Q costs 24 Strain, interrupts the elder and extends cooling to 18 seconds. Timeout is recoverable. Alternatively defeat the actual tagged optional elder at yard plus `(1000,1500)`. Defeat receipt retries failed saves.
4. For the ten new choices, E maintenance requires three turns two seconds apart. R/T shows the exact 60-Strain permanent tradeoff and requires the same key here within six seconds. Cinderhold reuses its previous Blackslag choice and will not charge again.
5. E at the loading point takes one unit from finite stock. The visible bundle follows the player and saved custody survives reload. Only one cargo can be held; every city's first shipment is available once.
6. Walk into the chosen bay. Service check is yard plus `(500,440)`; sheltered recess check is plus `(100,1040)`. E seals the local check. The hand route's service gate closes at night; wait until day. No time failure erases cargo.
7. Use the existing travel network to the registry's receiving city. E at its receiving shelf, yard plus `(-300,-350)`, increments destination stock, records the arrival day and clears custody. No new realm access or teleport is granted.
8. Tell the actual sender on return. Other local or receiving-city residents can acquire a personally attributed account through real conversation. Remote residents do not acquire it.
9. Return to the sender's authored threshold while that resident is physically home and visible, usually at night. E saves the reunion and plays the return scene. Each other resident also has a unique inspectable threshold and personal memory after a real report. Bes keeps his original home; urgent walkout, material and hazard duties override ordinary night schedules.
