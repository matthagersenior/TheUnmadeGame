2. From hub travel through the correct connections. Portal G must refuse unsupported attunement, including late-realm destinations, and accept the correct earned level. Return routes must match `AtlasPassages`.
3. Confirm spawn on southern terrain, not in void. Before the first intervention the northern platform cannot be walked onto. Inspect either trial clue, operate the realm-specific mechanism, verify **only** the selected bridge becomes collidable, and walk over the actual gap.
4. Return to keeper; begin a distinct aftermath arc. Inspect the second control on the selected span and the branch-specific clue beyond it, then obtain the **matching** witness. Wrong witnesses, unreachable markers, and mismatched F7/F8 must fail clearly.
5. At the final decision, verify two deliberate presses, one open gate, one sealed gate, and the correctly reconstructed communal destination on the far side. Check no alternate ground path avoids the meaningful gate.
6. Save/reload at every stage, including before visiting, after travel, after alternate trial clue, after control, after first return, after witness and after final choice. Verify all six realm snapshots coexist and do not overwrite Bellwold/Saltwake/Cinderhold.
7. Corrupt-array and SaveGame failure injection must reject unsafe progress. Repeated interactions do not duplicate rewards or landmarks. Defeat in any of six regions returns the player to **that same** safe southern platform.
8. Verify locality: only nearby eyewitnesses know first-hand events; others attribute rumor. Apply real navigation, art, accessible map, controller bindings and meaningful authored traversal/combat/puzzles before claiming a polished RPG.
