# Civic Reverberation — pre-Unreal source and acceptance

## Material consequences tied to actual custody

The Hearth Petition and Cinderhold wage record remain independent. The
Cinderhold -> Saltwake -> Crossings relay is source state that cannot
jump ahead of physical in-person interactions. Civic Reverberation reads
only the already saved petition relay and Bes household record, and never
creates an additional save counter.

When stage >= 2, Saltwake's **Civic.SaltwakeQuestion** becomes visible and
inspectable after Mera has received the petition. When stage 4, Orrel's
real receipt determines **one** exclusive Crossings affordance:
**Civic.PrivateShelterPassage** (collision-enabled screened side deck)
or **Civic.PublicEvidencePlatform** (public redacted investigative station).
Both are physically inspectable, optional, and independent of the main-road
gate or reward systems. Invisible actors have collision disabled.

The Unanswered Road builds an optional question from the already witnessed
reply: what if a protected person and a public debt are both real? This
questions Nhal-Vey's one-history demand without changing boss availability
or world resolution.

Mera and Orrel can refer only to the delivered letter. The player journal
can reflect the real current household account, including a later lawful
repayment; neither resident automatically learns it. An additional delivery
must be separately implemented before they can discuss new wages.

## Portable assertions

- Crossings effects absent without Orrel receipt, including after Mera alone
- Private and public affordances mutually exclusive
- Local Saltwake response absent before Mera's real receipt
- Household settlement changes the player's account, not physical route
- Distinct branch questions address Nhal-Vey without granting abilities
- Every local model returns no outcome for invalid/absent custody
- Native tests assert live actors, line-of-sight, collision gating, NPC
  knowledge boundaries, the main-story journal and saved parent fields

## Engine-only acceptance

UE 5.8 UHT build; actor positions and playable collision at each of the
three named places; private/public route visibility after reload; no block
to the original path; two-sided NPC dialogue context; old profile without
petitions; post-repayment world journal consistency; physically traversed
three-realm loop; VO/subtitles, controller interaction and performance.

No new finished level, audio track or Unreal binary is asserted here.
