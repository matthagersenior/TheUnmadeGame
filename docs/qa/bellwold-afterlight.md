# Bellwold Afterlight — Engine acceptance checklist

GitHub CI covers native C++17 behavior and Python source wiring. Unreal compilation and gameplay remain unverified.

- Complete the original lantern delivery and the three-witness Refuge Compact. Then return and talk to Hessa. Before these achievements, Afterlight must be inaccessible.
- Inspect both uniquely tagged evidence markers with E. Verify proximity, line-of-sight, stable collision and that nearby unrelated NPCs cannot mask the action.
- Follow the relief cache to Sorin or the censored census to Ivera. Other NPCs must not advance the same chapter.
- Inspect the other evidence before meeting a witness. The route may switch only during investigation, not after the testimony is recorded.
- At Hessa, F7 may commit shelter after Sorin; F8 may publish names after Ivera. Incompatible endings give feedback without deleting evidence.
- The returned ward and census objects are exclusive, physically visible and persist across save/reload. No double landmarks or invisible collision.
- Confirm the earned armor and charm are mutually exclusive; after multiple reloads, each player receives exactly the one item they earned.
- Compare local NPC witnessed versus rumor observations, their separate trust and fear responses, and saved reactions after travel.
- Test absent old save flags and all corrupt Afterlight stage, approach, and outcome integers. Corruption must never silently replace prior choices.
- Replace debug-only keyboard controls with a discoverable and accessible journal, confirmation screen, controller bindings and remappable actions.
- Add actual shelter capacity, resource scarcity, a second-night encounter, distinctive architecture, weather, sound and optional follow-on Paperhaven consequences as subsequent content. Do not claim these are presently implemented.
