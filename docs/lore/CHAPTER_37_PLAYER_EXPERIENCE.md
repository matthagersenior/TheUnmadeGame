## Chapter 37 The Rooms We Keep

A road becomes a home through the rooms it lets you return to. The Unmade already has nine realms, named people, remembered disputes and choices that cannot be quietly taken back. This chapter brings their everyday spaces closer to their adventures. An abandoned wing can reopen a work table; a repaired table can make a later conversation possible; a public record can change who waits at a doorway. None of these changes explains the origin of the world or appoints a single ending for every traveler.

These additions extend the earlier adventures. The six registered chambers, their evidence and captives, the boss outcomes, Bes’s household, Varda’s fieldwork and every established wage or promise remain. A traveler can finish an earlier road without entering its new wing. The optional rooms ask for attention rather than obedience, and their rewards offer different ways to travel and fight.

### 37.1 The rooms beyond the familiar route

Each dungeon now has a three-room wing connected to an existing chamber. The production atlas shows all nine layouts; the player’s map records only places they have actually discovered. The earlier chamber numbers retain their meaning. A new passage is an addition to their history, not an excuse to erase it.

| City | Optional wing | Fieldwork demand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crossings | Lantern Survey Gallery | Walk with a consenting guide through three bearings |
| Saltwake | Drowned Pump Annex | Read the pump stations at low water; shelter on the dry ledge |
| Cinderhold | Cooling Ledger Foundry | Complete three relays within twenty seconds |
| Drevlach | Broken Oath Court | Guard at oath stones and hear a wounded duelist |
| Orravane | Choir Lift Observatory | Reach the elevated tuning stations |
| Vathless | Seam Memory Archive | Compare a paired record and return with low strain |
| Eillun | Unobserved Index Vault | Read the lamps while crouched and unobserved |
| Tharniv | Ungrafted Root Nursery | Apply an earned Living Roads chapter to the barrier |
| Auvren | Threshold Counterweight Hall | Reach the moving lift above a safe lower floor |

In the Lantern Survey Gallery, the field worker must first agree to accompany the traveler. The guide follows the path the traveler has just walked and waits when left behind. Their presence at a bearing matters; arriving alone does not complete the same task. A person frightened enough to refuse is not ordered to cooperate by the quest journal. The route can be tried again through a real conversation and a safer relationship.

Saltwake’s annex asks the traveler to read a place in time. The low-water interval permits the pump readings; a later surge punishes lingering in the exposed center. The dry ledge remains a way to watch the cycle. A missed crossing is not a permanent failure. In Cinderhold, an incomplete cooling sequence expires and can be restarted. A completed sequence is remembered. The foundry needs a careful return, not a claim that heat can be solved by saying the right words.

Drevlach’s oath stones lead to an actual duelist. Guarding is a posture the traveler must hold, and restraint becomes possible only when the living opponent is sufficiently wounded. The duelist can yield; death is also a durable outcome. Later people distinguish those events. The oath does not absolve a kill merely because the traveler eventually finds a useful record.

Orravane’s ledges require climbing to the missing voice. Vathless sends the traveler back to a record already seen, now beside its counterpart. Eillun’s Index Lamp Sentry patrols around the quiet reading. Its real presence can be avoided, or its actual defeat can be remembered; clearing that threat remains a legitimate alternative. Tharniv’s barrier responds to an earned chapter of Living Roads. Auvren’s counterweight changes height above a lower floor that catches a missed crossing. These are different demands on movement, memory, timing, restraint and learned power.

Every wing has a named guardian and a reward station. The actual guardian must be resolved before the reward can be claimed. A tool earned afterward opens a physical shortcut, allowing the return to feel shorter because something has changed. The source guardians reuse the realm’s signature combat patterns at reduced health. The rooms, ramps, gates and lift remain primitive construction that must be inspected in Unreal before they can be called finished environments.

### 37.2 The weapon you chose

A traveler should not need to guess which weapon the world believes they carry. The active weapon and armor now have separate selections. Choosing a realm weapon replaces the previous weapon’s slot bonuses; choosing an armor replaces its slot. A charm remains a charm. Passive equipment techniques and actively learned techniques are shown separately, so a useful enchantment is not confused with a different blade.

The inventory compares owned equipment with the active slot and shows whether a change raises or lowers its attack or defense. Earlier equipment remains owned. Old saves adopt their previously selected expedition kit when no newer loadout has been recorded. Stable item identities and earned claims are preserved. A failed equip write does not erase the reward that has already been honestly won; the traveler can select it later.

Combat also gains an interval between intention and contact. Stamina is reserved when the attack begins. The blade then crosses a bounded contact window, and cover and facing matter at the moment it can reach an opponent. A single swing does not strike again merely because an animation event repeats. A miss still costs effort. The animation rate follows the weapon’s rhythm, with an optional contact notify for imported clips and a timed spatial fallback in source.

Marksmen and Watchers send traveling shots toward their warned aim. The shot can meet a wall, another body, or a defended traveler; it does not pursue a target around a corner. The redacted focus gains the same physical journey for its attack. Guard, parry and evasion share identified impact receipts. Directional injury feedback indicates where a melee blow came from, and stagger resistance prevents a strong opponent from being held in an endless interruption.

These changes establish source behavior. They do not prove that a weapon socket, imported clip, camera, sound cue or collision shape already feels right in the engine. That proof belongs to the deferred playtest, including slower frame rates and crowded encounters. The original ten reality disciplines still retain their own earned conditions and consequences.

### 37.3 A table after the return

All twenty-nine settlements gain local service rooms. Their primitive furniture gives a counter, a work table, a bed and a reading or hearing area an actual place. The normal rooms open from six in the morning until ten at night. Cinderhold maintains continuous furnace shifts; Eillun’s archive works through the night. A closed counter is a condition the traveler can understand and wait through, not a silent refusal from a button.

The local specialties follow the realm. Lantern bearings help orient the next journey. Salt purification restores strained attention. Heat tempering briefly strengthens an attack. Mediation enters the local social record. Choir tuning offers a short evasive opening. Seam recovery clears a temporary status. Quiet indexing gives a brief quiet lens. Rootwork tends an injury, and threshold guidance supplies a temporary ward. The earned privilege permits two uses in a realm, once per game day, shared between its settlements. Traveling to the next counter does not manufacture fresh stock.

A protected reserve opens the shared beds. Public witness opens the hearing and preserves the road claimant already established in the earlier adventure. A repaired work table clears visible rubble and gives the field worker and returned residents a place to resume their routines. Their movement remains subject to collision and personal reactions. Entry waypoints help them approach the actual door rather than walk straight through its walls; authored navigation still needs engine acceptance.

Repair does not create a wage payment. In Cinderhold, a restored table leaves Bes’s balance, Mera’s consent, Varda’s account and the household’s obligations intact. “The repaired work table does not settle anyone’s wages,” the local account insists. “We still have to account for the shift.” The traveler’s new insight is a different resource, and no repair can quietly spend someone else’s earned pay.

A public accord can later gain shared access through mediation. The table must first be repaired, and the actual witness must meet the traveler again on a different day. The resulting access is a new fact. It does not replace the accord, erase the claimant, make disagreement disappear or appoint the public choice as the only right ending. A door can reopen while a conversation remains difficult.

### 37.4 People who recognize the day

The forty-five expedition residents now keep recognition across distinct game days. Repeating a greeting does not create a lifetime of friendship. Four conditional scenes in each realm connect the field worker’s introduction, the guardian witness, the disputed accord and the rescued people’s return. The earlier household and resident histories continue beside them.

Each new scene needs its real speaker nearby. It is recorded before the gesture or caption begins, and it can earn its scene reward only once. A caption remains visible for a reading interval based on its length. Existing personal dialogue is retained and extended, rather than exchanged for a regional summary. The game’s optional local dialogue model cannot grant scene progress, rewards or consent.

The field worker’s first scene makes the optional task personal. The witness’s later scene recognizes the reported guardian outcome. The leader’s returning conversation places the accord beside the repaired or disputed room. The rescued residents’ scene can occur only after the actual release and return conditions have been met. These scenes do not claim that a remote resident watched a fight they could not see.

Reserve, witness, repair and mediation events also enter the existing local memory system through nearby observers and clear sight. Trust and fear continue to affect native behavior. A witnessed repair can matter differently from a rumor of it. These additions do not overwrite old memories or create omniscient global reputation. The OPEN mysteries remain questions with bounded evidence, not rewards for accumulating familiarity.

### 37.5 Rewards that make a choice

Resolving a wing and claiming its return gives five insight. Four conditional relationship scenes can provide four more. Insight is finite and its accounting is exact. It does not replace local favor, trade marks, professions, wages or the original item economy.

The Duelist build adds attack while reducing armor and raising stamina cost. The Warden adds defense while lowering attack and movement speed. The Pathfinder extends reach and movement at a higher attack cost. A first build costs two insight; changing its type costs one. An already owned realm build can be reequipped freely, without changing the chosen weapon or learned technique. A traversal tool, crafted binding, local privilege or repair costs one. The journal shows these costs before spending, so an experiment can be a considered choice.

A tool opens the earned wing shortcut. A crafted binding adds two armor to the active realm build and extends a field kit’s danger suppression from twenty to thirty seconds. A privilege opens shared access and its bounded local specialty. Repair restores the work table and can support later mediation. These benefits affect routes, fighting, recovery and relationships rather than differing only in a reward label.

The new optional experience record validates discoveries, progress, scene prerequisites, day limits and spending against the earlier expedition and personal history. Failed writes restore state, relay attempts and world time before effects or rewards. Old saves have no obligation to contain the new fields. A damaged record is protected from being replaced by invented progress.

### 37.6 A journal with room to breathe

The interface now separates objectives, people and returns, evidence, the ten disciplines, and economy. A tracked objective stays visible while traveling. The map records discovered cities, visited chambers and completed wing stations; it does not display unseen room coordinates or personal options that have not been offered and heard. Choosing a map destination sets a bearing, not a teleport or a completed quest.

Settings provide adjustable text, camera sensitivity, sound volume, high contrast, reduced flashes, tutorial visibility and keyboard or controller prompts. Attack, guard, dodge, interaction and the learned technique can be rebound independently for keyboard and controller. Reserved and conflicting controls are refused with an explanation. Menu pages expose their own action groups and controller navigation, with gameplay input excluded while the menu is open.

A bed can pass six game hours after a second confirmation. The world’s obligations remain due. Recovery after defeat selects the nearest earned city’s safe platform without resetting story, inventory or people’s memories. Neither resting nor defeat appoints a different protagonist or a preferred ending.

The full native interface has not been rendered or playtested in Unreal. Font scaling, focus, capture, scrolling, controller behavior, moving support, navigation and every contact frame remain part of the engine acceptance. The downloadable editions preserve the complete earlier books and illustrations. They describe implemented source, portable checks and explicit production limits; they do not substitute for the first time the traveler actually walks these rooms.
