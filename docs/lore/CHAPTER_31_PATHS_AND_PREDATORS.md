# Volume X Chapter 31

## Paths and predators

Playable source v11, 11 October 2026. Engine compilation and playtesting remain outstanding.

### 31.1 The route someone maintained

Orren had not asked for a hero. He pointed to the west side of the fossil ribs, where a screen concealed a narrow route. The marks on its latch were ordinary wear. Someone had opened it often enough to polish the metal. Beyond it, the ribs offered cover from the road's beasts. That did not explain who had first built the passage, or which remembered road it had served.

The six field surveys begin with that kind of material observation. At Ash Road, Glass Cut, Forgotten Bells, Torn Archive, Saltglass Quay and Blackslag Field, the player can operate an actual nearby latch with E. A collision-bearing screen withdraws only after the optional survey state has saved successfully. The route has a floor and an outer wall; it supplies another approach and physical cover, rather than promising invisibility or teleportation. A visible instruction then asks the player to return to the local scout.

The survey is complete only when the player physically returns and presses E within speaking distance and clear sight. Orren marks a maintained footpath for travelers. Vesh recognizes a shutter that offers shelter from the watchers. Deryn identifies a bell service passage. Isti records a keeper's latch without inventing its missing owner. Halven recognizes a covered landing for the net crews. Varda records a kiln screen while expressly separating that practical work from Bes's hearth and unpaid wages. These are bounded statements about observed routes. None resolves an OPEN mystery.

### 31.2 Twelve ways to approach danger

The names on the road now correspond to twelve different source profiles. Cinderjaw fights close with a readable jaw strike. Pale Strider closes faster and reaches farther, but carries less health. Veilwatch holds a longer pulse distance; Gleam Moth is lighter and faster, with a shorter flash reach. Hollow Hound presses close with frequent bites. Grief Lantern advances slowly and warns for longer before its stronger peal. Inkmaw trades speed for a heavier, slower crush. Redaction Eye seeks distance and a view past the shelves. Saltglass Crawler is durable and slow. Drowned Watch holds a longer pulse lane. Ashmaw has the heaviest ordinary strike and a long warning. Kiln Wisp is quick and comparatively fragile.

These differences are health, damage, locomotion speed, reach, retreat distance, cooldown and warning duration in real combat source. The player receives creature-specific counterplay text. Pillars, shelves and walls interrupt sight; guarding remains an available physical defense. The optional imported beast and wraith rigs still use the prior shared performance families. Species-specific bodies, new attack animation clips, projectiles, positional sound and balanced authored boss arenas have not been produced in this pass.

Each ground has one elder, the third encounter placement. It has increased health and damage but also longer warning and recovery intervals. Below half health it rallies and moves faster, with a distinct rally warning before a strike. The rally never refills health or grants an immediate invisible hit. These are six optional miniboss-style combat encounters using existing hostile actors, not six new narrative bosses or changes to Nhal-Vey. Encounter defeat remains session-local under the existing combat contract; loading a world can recreate the enemy. The survey passage and completed report persist independently.

### 31.3 The breath between intention and impact

Fold now has a second tactical use against an enemy already winding up. The existing player cast pays its existing Strain cost and exposes the foe as before; its exposure callback cancels that pending strike and gives the foe one second of recovery. There is no second free cast or new resource pool. An enemy outside its wind-up is still exposed, but does not receive an invented interrupted attack. Ordinary strikes also have a brief recovery interval, giving the player time to reposition before another warning begins.

Routine fracture use remains reversible. This extension does not add an irreversible Rewrite, change the ten disciplines, grant mastery, award quest gear or bypass a campaign requirement. It adds another understandable relationship between a visible enemy intention and a familiar ability. The eventual animation and sound pass must make that relationship readable without relying on overhead text alone.

### 31.4 A return that survives departure

The optional fieldwork has three saved stages per ground: unopened, passage opened awaiting report, and reported. Its separate versioned save holds precisely six bounded stages. Missing data starts a new optional survey; malformed or unsupported existing data is preserved and further survey writes are disabled. A failed save leaves the stage and screen unchanged so the player can retry. No survey operation touches the established campaign save, municipal money, relief baskets, evidence, Mera's consent or the player's inventory.

Returning to a completed scout repeats the earned local acknowledgment without incrementing progress, minting payment or issuing equipment. The screen remains withdrawn after a reload. Residents retain their existing stable identities. These six conversations are fixed authored reports rather than a newly claimed full dialogue-tree or relationship simulation.

### 31.5 Production acceptance

The source test suite checks profile counterplay and source wiring. Unreal acceptance must still compile the new SaveGame class through UHT, walk every side route, inspect its visibility rays and NPC interaction competition, attempt every latch, report and reload each survey, and simulate failed or malformed saves. Both beast and watcher elders must be fought across their rally threshold. Fold must cancel a warned strike without applying damage at the cancelled deadline. Guard, cover, defeat and checkpoint recovery must remain usable.

The six existing scouts, twelve profiles, six elders, six passage mechanisms and six optional two-action surveys expand the source game. This edition supplies no compiled executable, rendered gameplay footage, new finished character art or remote Unreal playtest. Both complete illustrated books retain their prior chapters and art, and this chapter records the exact source increment without canonizing an ending.
