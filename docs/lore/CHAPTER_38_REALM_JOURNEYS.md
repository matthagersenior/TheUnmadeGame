## Chapter 38 Nine Realm Journeys

Each realm now has an explicit source path from its city board through the frontier, registered dungeon chambers, objective resolution, boss, witnessed return and equipment choice. The personal accord and optional fieldwork remain separate adventures with their own saved facts. This chapter closes gaps in those connections. It preserves the previous nine realms and their people; it does not appoint a new protagonist, explain the OPEN mysteries or select one campaign ending.

### 38.1 Finding the next room

The six-chamber layouts and their three-room wings remain intact. Walking physically inside a chamber now registers its arrival before its encounters can accept attacks. A player can still inspect the doorway. Arrival requires the earlier chamber and, for the arena, the chosen objective branch. Crossing a wall through a debug command does not create the missing history.

This matters to both combat and memory. An opponent defeated before its room was recorded could previously leave a victory without a valid receipt. Ordinary opponents and the arena boss now use the same entered-room authority as their defeat record. Encounter activation, the saved kill, restored patrol state and the next objective agree about what the traveler actually reached. A completed boss outcome still stands down local patrols; a living surrender remains visible and cannot be attacked again.

The directional signs follow the authored turns rather than assuming every next room lies north. Five signs in each main dungeon point toward the next numbered chamber. The earned return walk marks its northern exit, eastern connector, southern road and western frontier approach. These are constructed signs on the existing primitive paths. Actual collision, capsule clearance, navigation and readability remain part of engine acceptance.

### 38.3 Choices the traveler can read

At the holding-chamber council, rescue and evidence remain two resolutions of the same expedition. Rescue requires both actual releases. Evidence requires all three records. The traveler may collect evidence and free people along either path, but the selected resolution is permanent. A living boss can accept the evidence route; combat remains possible. Dead opponents cannot be revived by presenting a later record.

The first branch interaction now previews what will be committed. Repeating the same action within twenty seconds confirms it. Choosing the other option changes the preview, and waiting lets it expire. This transient preview never becomes a saved branch. The weapon-or-armor reward uses the same confirmation, names the exact item and base slot benefit, and makes its one-choice limit visible. A save failure leaves the underlying choice uncommitted. Already earned equipment can still be reequipped without a fresh irreversible decision.

The frontier return needs the real leader within range and an unobstructed line of sight. A nearby person behind a wall no longer counts as witnessing the return. Rescued people still return only after their actual releases and the committed safe return. The record does not claim an animated captive escort where the source still uses a saved relocation.

### 38.4 A journal that follows the journey

The tracker derives sixteen stages from the existing expedition, personal-quest and fieldwork records. It distinguishes the board from the frontier, individual unentered chambers from objective resolution, the boss from the return, and a witnessed return from an unclaimed reward. It names the local leader and witness when their actual conversations are next. It also distinguishes the personal offer, account, physical evidence and accord.

The optional wing has its own entrance, three fieldwork stations, guardian and claim station. A guardian's defeat does not silently claim the final reward. When those stages are resolved, the tracker identifies the journey as resolved and offers returning conversations, upgrades, repairs and mediation as optional work. It does not demand every finite purchase, convert familiar people into completion counters or force a single ethical ending.

The adventure journal now resolves the actual player equipment component before reading its modifiers. It no longer assumes the old single expedition kit is still the current weapon and armor. Equipment remains the authority for those independent slots and their passive technique; the learned active technique remains separately selected.

### 38.5 What the source verification establishes

The composed portable test follows fifty-four combinations across the nine realms: rescue with combat, evidence with a living surrender, both personal accords, and evidence journeys that release the captives only after the boss stands down. It restores the expedition, personal and experience records together at 1,944 checkpoints. It checks encounter eligibility, finite insight and service uses, permanent rewards, remembered scenes, repairs, mediation and refusal of corrupted progress. A separate session carries rewards through all nine realms to check that a later selection leaves earlier earned equipment intact.

The existing campaign, ten disciplines, later-realm chapters, ordinary combat, household, economy and consequence tests remain part of the full verification suite. Portable tests establish those source contracts; they do not prove a camera feels right, a doorway fits the character, a moving platform supports them or a controller focus path works in a viewport.

Unreal compilation, asset import, rendered interface review and engine playtesting remain deferred. The release preserves the full previous illustrated books and prototype assets. A source journey is now more coherent and better checked, while its rooms, performances and encounters still require the first real engine walkthrough before they can be called a finished playable world.
