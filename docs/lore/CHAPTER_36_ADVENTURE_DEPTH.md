## Chapter 36 What A Road Asks Of You

A road becomes familiar when it gives a traveler something to recognize and something to reconsider. The same room repeated nine times cannot carry the weight of nine lands. This chapter gives the existing adventures different routes, different demands on the body, and local questions that remain difficult after the boss is gone. The people already waiting at the frontier and refuge become the witnesses of those questions.

### 36.1 Nine ways through danger

The nine dungeons now follow distinct six-chamber paths. Their doors join the chambers that actually stand beside one another. A boss gate guards the real approach to its arena, and an outer return walkway connects the earned northern exit back to inhabited ground. The room numbers remain the same saved discoveries even where their positions change.

The optional side passages ask for more than another set of valve lights. At Crossings, the road bells must be rung first, third, second. Saltwake's outer pumps must be set during the low-water interval. Cinderhold's three cooling relays must be reached before eighteen seconds pass. Drevlach weighs two and three against five. Orravane makes the traveler climb to the upper walkway before grounding its wind anchors. Vathless asks for memory shutters in order. Eillun asks for three readings without a living guard watching, though defeating the patrol also removes its watch. Tharniv grows its root controls third, first, second. Auvren leaves the origin mirror dark and lights its two boundaries.

A mistake can be tried again. These side challenges open physical alcoves; they do not take the main campaign hostage. Each completed route grants two local favor. A crafted field kit can open the named cache beyond it, adding two more favor and the evidence of exploration needed for the final mastery lesson. The earlier survey valves remain available beside the new challenges.

The present structures are graybox implementations. An upper walkway has collision and a height check in source, but has not been climbed in an engine test. The root controls do not yet perform a finished growth animation, and a timed pump puzzle is not a completed water simulation. Those distinctions remain visible in the production record.

### 36.2 The person who disagrees with you

A frontier leader offers the new personal story after the traveler arrives. A refuge resident supplies the testimony. A separate physical record in the fourth chamber gives that testimony something the player can examine. Only after the expedition boss is resolved can the traveler return and commit an accord. The same choice must be confirmed beside the real leader; a menu cannot decide for a distant person.

Velka's voluntary promise survives beneath a collector's forced margin. Deyra can expose both without pretending that exposure feeds the families relying on the first promise. ### 36.3 A choice changes the road after it

A protected reserve earns four local favor and cheaper provisions. A public witness earns three favor and cheaper lessons. The leader and witness remember the accord in different authored return lines. Those prices express a local alliance; they do not overwrite the older citizen-memory model or its knowledge boundaries.

Public witness also brings opposition onto the outer return road. The Charter Enforcer, Brine Syndic, Reservoir Keeper, Margin Collector, Anchor Auditor, Seam Evictor, Index Hunter, Boundary Hewer and Registry Sealkeeper each contest one realm's public accord. They are conditional opponents rather than another mandatory delivery. A traveler may avoid their territory. An actual defeat earns two local favor, is saved, and will not be undone by leaving and returning. A failed save retries the receipt without announcing a victory that was not recorded.

The protected reserve avoids that encounter. Its quieter return does not mean that every person agrees with the choice. The folded names, private meter, sealed oath and household boundary remain things another resident can question. Consequences include safety and service costs as well as an extra fight.

### 36.4 A weapon changes the work of fighting

The expedition weapons now differ in reach, rhythm, stamina cost and finishing effect. The Kilnfang hammer is slower and heavier than the Elseborn saber. The Breakwater spear reaches farther than a short blade. The Downfall glaive controls a longer approach. Three strikes within the combination window strengthen the finishing hit and briefly stagger the target; the weapon can also slow, burn, weaken or expose it.

Stamina begins at one hundred. Guarding drains it. Ordinary recovery restores it, and earned mastery improves recovery. Dodge spends twenty-two stamina and provides a brief evasion window while the character moves through the collision-aware movement system. A guard raised at the right time can parry, spending five stamina and staggering the attacker. Repeatedly raising the guard has its own recovery interval. A defended attack or hazard pulse is remembered as a receipt, so it cannot simply apply again when the evasion ends.

Enemies also have reason to stand together. A guarding sentinel supplies nearby allies with armor. Realm groups vary in their combinations of sentinel, skirmisher, marksman and disruptor. No more than two expedition warnings begin concurrently in a group. Crouching reduces distant detection, while physical sight and facing still matter. Burn wounds expire, slow affects movement, and stagger cancels attack patterns. These are local deterministic rules, not decisions made by a language model.

The original bosses keep their authored signature footprints. Weapon strikes remain the existing native reach-and-sight attacks; a final animation-driven hitbox system and physical projectiles have not been established. Revival now preserves outgoing swing numbering so old enemy receipts cannot invalidate the recovering player's future strikes. The earlier duplicate guard declaration is also corrected before the deferred Unreal build.

### 36.5 Services that belong to a settlement

Provisioners, field-kit benches and mastery teachers now have physical stations in all twenty-nine existing settlements. Their balances and limited stock are shared by realm, so a traveler cannot reset a purchase by walking to the next town. A provisioner offers four purchases in total, no more than one per game day, and does not charge someone at full health. A crafted kit opens a cache or grounds an unresolved danger field for twenty seconds. A surveyed or already quiet field does not consume it.

Three mastery lessons change stamina recovery, parry and dodge. The second requires the local route; the third requires the cache. Both personal-story branches can reach all three levels with a crafted cache kit, though spending favor on supplies changes what remains available. The old marks, wages, professions, recipes and sanctuary services retain their own rules. A new bench does not pretend that a complete inn interior or career system has been produced.

An earned equipment technique can be selected and invoked separately from the original ten disciplines. Marked Opening exposes a target; Pressure Read slows it; Ember Counter burns; Oath Interrupt weakens. Grounded Fold interrupts, Seam Read staggers and exposes, Unlisted Step provides evasion, Root Counter heals an injured guarding player, and Nearness Hold wards incoming damage for six seconds. Each costs thirty stamina and has a twelve-second recovery. These uses give equipment progression something more specific to do than add another number.

### 36.6 Reading the world while playing it

A native player interface now builds health and stamina bars, strain and combat feedback, visible-target health and warnings, and context prompts. Journal, equipment and realm-map panels scroll. Equipment and recovery controls have buttons. Map selection supplies bearings and chamber coordinates without teleporting the player. Personal choices show a preview and require confirmation beside the named leader.

Ordinary dialogue subscribes to the existing authored conversation event. The earlier directed-scene subtitle widgets remain in use. Opening a menu pauses the local world and preserves its own input-state changes until closure. Wrapped actions, keyboard focus and Escape closure are supplied in source. Earlier gameplay notices now reach the local interface and its bounded recent-event history; initialization without a local viewport keeps a diagnostic fallback, and no engine screenshot or completed shipping accessibility assessment is claimed by this chapter.

The three original rigs now carry nineteen clips each, fifty-seven total. Dodge and Parry join the earlier performance set. Offline previews show walking extremes as well as middle and end poses. The prior sounds and all preceding illustrations remain in the package. These are low-poly prototype motion sources, not motion capture or an imported animation graph.

The optional depth record contains one hundred and ten integers. It validates progress, local spending, training, conditional threats and selected techniques against the existing expedition and game day. Old saves may omit it. Failed writes restore permanent progress and temporary challenge state before applying recovery or opening a gate. Partial puzzle progress, stamina and timed statuses remain session state.

This edition extends both complete illustrated masters without discarding their earlier pages. Portable tests and source validators cover the new rules. Unreal compilation, widget rendering, collision, navigation, sound and animation timing, combat balance and playtesting remain deferred. The next engine acceptance must judge how these authored differences feel under the player's hands.
