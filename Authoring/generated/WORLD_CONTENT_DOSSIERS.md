## Nine cultures and their production identities

### The Wayward Charter — ThreefoldReach

**Built environment:** Stitched timber, refugee bells, chalk waystones and argument-plastered inns
**Ethic:** A witnessed promise outranks an inherited seal.
**Everyday custom:** At first light two neighbors reread a road's name aloud, including those denied the original charter.
**Audio:** Struck copper bells, wagon-wheel rests and overlapping spoken distances
**Art / accessibility:** Mist gold and ink blue; stable landmarks before every camera turn
**Noncombat activity:** Repair a communal mile marker using testimony, never repeated commodity grinding
**Living conflict:** Shelter requires trust; records demand difficult public consent

### The Rainward Account — WidowedRain

**Built environment:** Hollow boat-roofs, ledgers hung like prayer flags, dry harbors full of tide tools
**Ethic:** Water belongs to its keepers only while they can prove it was shared.
**Everyday custom:** A departing sailor leaves a dry cup on a sill for a sea not yet returned.
**Audio:** Rain counted on glass, wet rope under wind, a bell with no answering buoy
**Art / accessibility:** Desaturated jade and pewter; reachable high ground and rainfall visibility options
**Noncombat activity:** Compare a chart with a first-hand shoreline witness
**Living conflict:** A broker sells remembered water to families who need actual rain

### The Ember Commons — HearthBeneath

**Built environment:** Carved heat channels, shared kilns and soot-marked kinship walls
**Ethic:** A fire is a promise to the next household, not hereditary property.
**Everyday custom:** Each street relights an empty hearth before feeding the communal flame.
**Audio:** Low stone resonance, breath, hammers and furnace air
**Art / accessibility:** Ember ochre, moss-black stone; non-flicker alternatives to fire flashes
**Noncombat activity:** Tend a heat channel and decide who receives warmth during shortages
**Living conflict:** Protecting an ember can become a justification for excluding outsiders

### The Estuary of Owed Days — TidalLedger

**Built environment:** Debt boats on foundations, inverted tide clocks, names bound into pier slats
**Ethic:** Nobody may pledge the tomorrow of a person who cannot consent.
**Everyday custom:** A lender writes a due date, then washes the book and preserves the erased impression.
**Audio:** Distant oar strokes without water, scratch of debt stylus, delayed bell
**Art / accessibility:** Black teal with silver date-stamps; rising-tide warning indicators
**Noncombat activity:** Audit a ferry manifest by meeting a borrower and examining a physical seal
**Living conflict:** Relief now may hide the architecture of generational exploitation

### The Downward Cantors — SkyBelow

**Built environment:** Ceiling streets, hanging stairs, counterweighted houses and public score walls
**Ethic:** No building should stand by erasing the person holding it up.
**Everyday custom:** Neighbors sing a support note to each other before ascending.
**Audio:** Voices above/below, stretched string harmonics, silent counted rests
**Art / accessibility:** Lavender slate with gold tuning points; optional static camera horizon
**Noncombat activity:** Help a family adjust a counterweight after observing its true load
**Living conflict:** Structural safety depends on forbidden unpaid labor

### The Seam-Walkers — CinderSpine

**Built environment:** Fossil ridges with family mortar, suspended quarry rails and layered graves
**Ethic:** Stone cut from history still belongs to the people erased with it.
**Everyday custom:** Quarry workers put a blank space in each wage ledger for missing ancestors.
**Audio:** Stone groans, dry percussion and voices trapped in rock strata
**Art / accessibility:** Ash-white and iron purple; cracks visibly widen before collapse
**Noncombat activity:** Make a safe brace from a documented material source
**Living conflict:** The mine supports homes while it un-makes the lineages that built them

### The Unentered Citizenry — HundredUnlived

**Built environment:** Addresses without numbers, consenting-door inscriptions, public thresholds with no census marks
**Ethic:** The right to be sheltered cannot depend on surrendering an origin.
**Everyday custom:** Hosts leave an unlabeled welcome cup and ask a guest how they wish to be addressed.
**Audio:** Soft door clicks, unsent letters, footfalls that answer before they occur
**Art / accessibility:** Fog cobalt and warm shelter amber; no flashing identity scan required
**Noncombat activity:** Help a guest reach shelter without giving up an identity
**Living conflict:** Recognition may expose the very people it is meant to protect

### The Untitled Courts — OrchardOfKings

**Built environment:** Root-grown bridges, vacant tribunals, communal fruit vaults and unoccupied crowns
**Ethic:** No law is just because its author wears a name.
**Everyday custom:** Gardeners recite dissenting votes before deciding where a communal root may grow.
**Audio:** Leaves tapping crowns, underground creaks and deliberate empty applause
**Art / accessibility:** Deep green and garnet with legible law marks on living bark
**Noncombat activity:** Regraft a public path by gaining consent from both sides
**Living conflict:** Refusing power can conceal power exercised without consent

### The Before-Witnesses — FirstAbsence

**Built environment:** Thresholds without foundations, overlapping doorways and contested origin altars
**Ethic:** More than one remembered beginning may be true without erasing any witness.
**Everyday custom:** Each newcomer is asked what they need, not which history they belong to.
**Audio:** Silent horizon pulses, doubled footfalls, an almost-remembered lullaby
**Art / accessibility:** Obsidian with contrasting pearl outlines; stable horizon setting mandatory
**Noncombat activity:** Compare two truthful maps, keeping a safe return path open
**Living conflict:** An imposed single origin would stabilize space by invalidating lives

## Ten canonical reality abilities / authored scene execution

### UnwriteLaw

**Existing training site:** Bellgrave
**World-space verb:** Briefly suspend a named local physics rule at a marked site; restore the rule automatically.
**Player feedback:** Ground-outline and sound fade before the duration expires.
**Fair limit:** High strain and limited duration; never delete saved history.
**Combination:** ParadoxConvergence
**Authored later-realm use:** Harmonic support, contradiction thresholds and localized sound silence

### Witnesscraft

**Existing training site:** EchoWell
**World-space verb:** Tag evidence as directly witnessed, rumored or unconfirmed before a testimony choice.
**Player feedback:** Three confidence glyphs, never an invisible morality color.
**Fair limit:** Cannot fabricate or force consent from any witness.
**Combination:** Cartography
**Authored later-realm use:** Civic records, competing choir accounts and personal memory conflicts

### BorrowedLives

**Existing training site:** PaperOrchard
**World-space verb:** Borrow an alternate professional capability temporarily without changing real identity.
**Player feedback:** Visible borrowed posture and temporary skill ribbon.
**Fair limit:** Cannot impersonate a living NPC or keep borrowed craft indefinitely.
**Combination:** LegacyForging
**Authored later-realm use:** Impossible ancestry masonry and personal origin testimony

### ParadoxConvergence

### Oathbinding

**Existing training site:** BellwoldRefuge

**Combination:** LivingRoads
**Authored later-realm use:** Public ferry, warm kiln and refusal of unlawful crown

**Existing training site:** SaltwakeHarbor
