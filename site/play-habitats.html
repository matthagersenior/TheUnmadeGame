Where the creatures live

Six provisional habitat demonstrations, based on existing encounter sites and canonical creatures. Explore tracks, use physical cover, disturb a territory, and inspect an alcove. This is an interactive browser graybox, not Unreal gameplay or admitted canon habitat placement.

Forgotten Bells

Walk toward the territory. Find cover.

Composure: 100 / 100

Movement creates tracks. The den responds to your presence.
WASD / arrows move · E or INTERACT inspects tracks · hide behind the stone cover

No habitat inspected.

What reacts: approaching the territory exposes tracks, brightens its warning signal and awakens the defender. Stone cover interrupts its attention. You can investigate an inspection marker and physically release a sheltered alcove. Approaching exposed for too long reduces composure and eventually returns you to safety; there are no infinite rewards or copied powers. These rules are implemented in the native C++ source too, but this browser scene is a separate demonstration and the Unreal implementation has not been compiled or playtested.
