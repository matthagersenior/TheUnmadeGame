# THE UNMADE
## A Field Codex for the Unanswered World
*Public field edition · 20 October 2026 · spoiler-light*

> Some worlds are broken. This one remembers being whole.

**An original dark, surreal, nonlinear RPG in development.** This is a reader's introduction to an authored universe, not an instruction manual for a released game. The world, peoples and rules described are source-backed designs; engine-tested gameplay and a packaged playable release are not yet available.

## 1. The World Is Not an Empty Stage

The places you cross existed before you arrived. People cook meals, tend lanterns, repair roads, argue about debts and choose who has a right to be remembered. The contradictions are physical: a bell with no last note, rain without the sea, heat borrowed from an impossible sun.

Discoveries can inform combat, a conversation, a practical repair, or a difficult choice. A witnessed fact is not the same as rumor. Helping someone does not grant ownership of their name or their story.

## 2. The Nine Realms

The nine-realm journey is nonlinear, but crossings are earned. The opening Reach precedes a choice of frontier, middle, and upper journeys before the later threshold. Visiting every optional activity is not secretly required.

### I — The Threefold Reach

**Bellwold · Paperhaven · The Crossings.** An interval where a bell fails to finish a note, streets disagree with records, and a refuge may predate its official history. The question is whether shelter and documents can both be respected when they conflict.

### II — The Widowed Rain

**Saltwake.** Dry piers are washed by real rain. Old shoreline impressions and wet ledgers tell different histories of the missing sea. One must learn where the storm is safe before deciding whose evidence remains public.

### III — The Hearth Beneath

**Cinderhold.** A mountain city shares a flame that does not belong to any known sun. Warmth passes through furnaces, valves, homes and obligations. Helping residents and uncovering a furnace's costs can require different forms of care.

### IV — The Owed Estuary

**Drevlach.** Time itself appears in contracts. Ferries and civic ledgers bear the price of futures no one has lived yet. A receipt may be real without its promise being ethical.

### V — The Inverted Choir

**Orravane.** Streets hang beneath the sky, and a city's songs help bear actual weight. Restoring a voice is not as simple as demanding someone sing.

### VI — The Yesterday Quarry

**Vathless.** Homes rest on stone containing histories removed from ordinary memory. Repair, ancestry, and the dignity of people whose names were cut away remain in conflict.

### VII — The Unclaimed City

**Eillun.** Doorways shelter people whose beginnings the census cannot affirm. The right to exist must not depend on exposing a protected identity.

### VIII — The Untitled Orchard

**Tharniv.** Roots embody laws nobody remembers consenting to. Empty thrones and communal stores ask whether an old order is legitimate simply because it survives.

### IX — The Before-Place

**Auvren.** Thresholds exist without foundations; sources disagree on what came first. The final destination is a question, not permission to rewrite every witness's truth.

## 3. The Principles of Learning

The intended progression follows **Observation → Understanding → Experimentation → Mastery**. See a technique, danger or anomaly in the world; gather credible evidence; try a bounded application; prove that you can use it responsibly. An observation does not automatically copy a monster's abilities or grant a permanent power.

The source codex contains many *studies*, rules and effects, not an equal number of directly castable combat abilities. Learning depends on canonical requirements, real opportunities and costs.

### Three basic reality techniques

- **Glimpse** reveals an authored alternate physical state or clue; it does not make an assertion true.
- **Fold** briefly alters a valid fracture and can expose a danger; it does not dissolve every obstacle.
- **Rewrite** confirms and persists a supported world variant; an irreversible choice requires clear preview and deliberate confirmation.

Other arts involve witness credibility, temporary borrowed skills, costly promises, disputed maps, shared roads and the discipline to understand an adversary rather than only defeat them.

## 4. The Ethics of Witnessing

A resident knows what they experienced, heard with provenance, or can reasonably infer. A character is not an omniscient quest marker. Private names remain private without consent; relief should not be traded for coerced exposure. Finite economies forbid conjuring stock, wages or deliveries out of nothing.

Conflicting accounts can remain meaningfully in conflict. **OPEN, DISPUTED and SEALED mysteries are not solved here.**

## 5. What Persists

The design aims for clearly previewed world changes, saved branches, differentiated homecomings and consequences people can physically witness. Optional Echo investigations, guardians, observatories, field recipes and personal chapters deepen the world without becoming hidden requirements for its main resolution.

The game preserves more than one legitimate final interpretation. **The Held Morning** and **The Many Mornings** remain distinct, supported possibilities, not a public claim of one universal answer.
