# THE UNMADE
## Gameplay Field Guide
*Public preview · spoiler-light · source design, not a verified playable build*

> Observe what exists. Decide what it means. Choose what you can live with. Return.

## Before You Begin

The Unmade is a third-person, dark-surreal RPG in production. This guide explains its **intended gameplay contract** using repository authoring and source rules, not an available game download. The actual Unreal Editor experience, platform inputs, collision, performance and campaign playtests remain unverified.

Your protagonist's identity is customizable. There is no single mandatory combat class and no requirement to resolve every optional mystery.

## 1. Explore an Impossible Place

Read the landscape before making a choice. Look for physically inspectable objects, accessible routes, ordinary resident activities, warning signs and contradictions between records. The best clue may be a cup left uncounted, a silent bell, a dated dockside receipt or an empty doorway.

Where a clue is discovered matters. The world distinguishes a direct observation from a story repeated by someone else. If a path looks blocked, inspect the environment and journal rather than assuming a hidden combat gate.

## 2. Investigate Through Evidence and Witnesses

1. Observe a real landmark or mechanism.
2. Compare different testimony or evidence.
3. Identify what is firsthand, interpreted, rumored or private.
4. Speak with residents without assuming they know every world event.
5. Take an action that reflects your understanding.

The intended design allows both care-centered and truth-centered resolutions, with deliberate consequences and access to an intelligible recovery path. Evidence does not authorize the exposure of someone's private identity.

## 3. Learn What the World Can Teach

The persistent learning loop is **Observe → Understand → Experiment → Master**. Seeing an enemy or supernatural principle makes it a *possible subject of study*, not a guaranteed collectible skill. Proven feats and stated prerequisites matter.

**Glimpse** can show alternate physical evidence. **Fold** changes a valid fracture briefly. **Rewrite** is reserved for supported, confirmed persistent world variations. Stronger arts have limits, costs and prerequisite knowledge. The ability codex also contains non-castable rules and narrative studies.

## 4. Fight by Reading the Threat

Combat design favors readable telegraphs, meaningful counterplay and recovery windows. Watch what a creature protects, repeats, fears or fails to understand. Research can offer additional opportunities with eligible threats or guardians, but not every encounter is pacifist and not every adversary teaches a usable power.

A strategy may combine equipment, bounded reality techniques, safe movement, and learned counters. Repeated grinding or completing optional bosses is not an intended gate to the main ending.

## 5. Craft, Trade and Protect Communities

Materials, wages, recipes, stock and services are finite. Before a purchase, repair, pledge or lasting intervention, the interface is intended to show the cost and consequence. Field crafts and regional rewards come from real acquisition routes; scarce resources do not appear again simply because the player reloaded a menu.

Helping a community may create a safe route, repair a public service, or change the way a resident greets you on return.

## 6. Choose a Nonlinear Journey

The journey opens in **the Threefold Reach**. From there, choose earned passages through frontier, middle and upper adventures; later story thresholds belong to **Auvren**. Nine realms exist, but optional errands, Echo arcs, guardians and observatories are not mandatory prerequisites for the main conclusion.

A journal and map should distinguish what you *know*, where you *could* go, and what you *chose not* to do. Uncertainty is part of the story, not permission for unusable objectives.

## 7. Make Lasting Choices Deliberately

Important irreversible choices require clear preview and confirmation. Witnessed deeds can influence residents, quest outcomes and physical features of a realm. Saved consequences should remain after departure and on return. A rumor does not make every NPC instantly react.

Neither The Held Morning nor The Many Mornings is presented as the universally correct resolution. Optional content remains optional in both cases.

## 8. Accessibility and Reliability Commitments

The game's source-level quality goals include subtitles for meaningful sound, readable visual hazard cues, text scale, reduced sensory effects, controller and keyboard access, recovery from failed interactions, and recoverable saves. These goals still need actual Unreal Editor and packaged-build acceptance tests.

## 9. Current Development Status

**Authored:** nine realm identities, quests and return design; canonical encyclopedia and stable IDs; source code for many RPG interactions; source-level validation.

**Not yet verified as shipped gameplay:** complete Unreal compilation, full rendered scenes, character assets and animation, final cinematic playback, accessible UI on target hardware, full progression playthroughs, and packaged releases.

There is **no public gameplay beta download** at present. Use the site's beta-interest form to volunteer for future testing and its feedback form to critique the cinematic concept trailer.
