WHAT THIS REALLY IS

A small interactive demonstration of a source-authored choice

Authentic foundation

The central 3D diorama is a real mesh generated by The Unmade's source code for the Threefold Reach. Bellwold belongs to that realm.

Accurate narrative dependency

Afterlight requires physical evidence, its matching witness, then Hessa's presence for a persistent choice. The two resolutions are mutually exclusive.

Clear limitations

This web prototype approximates walking and talking. It is not the Unreal executable, not the final Bellwold map, and not a demonstration of finished animation, combat AI, saves or UI.

This scene starts after the earlier lantern and Refuge Compact arcs for demonstration purposes. The browser remembers your illustrative choice locally, not in the game's canonical saved data. The Threefold Reach map is a miniature realm diorama, so character placement, distances, and the buildings shown are not the final level layout.
