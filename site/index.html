01 / THE PREMISE

Reality is an argument.
You are its witness.

In The Unmade, roads disagree with maps, bells remember sounds they never made, and entire communities live inside the consequences of forgotten decisions.

01

No single path through the world.

Begin at the Threefold Reach, then choose earned crossings across distinct realms. Optional adventures enrich the story but do not secretly gate its conclusion.

02

Knowledge must be earned.

Observe an enemy, a ritual, or an impossible place. Understand it, experiment with its rules, and master only what the world actually allows.

03

Your decisions leave witnesses.

Help a family, reveal a history, spare a guardian, rebuild a road. People respond to what they saw or credibly learned—not an all-knowing reputation score.

02 / AN ATLAS OF ABSENCE

Nine realms.
Nine different impossibilities.

Each realm has a civilization, a physical contradiction, people with lives beyond the quest, and consequences worth returning to. Select a realm to discover its premise—without major story spoilers.

03 / HOW IT PLAYS — DESIGN INTENT

Fight. Investigate.
Witness. Become.

⌁

Reality, with resistance

Learn constrained techniques such as Glimpse, Fold, and Rewrite. Powerful interventions require context and lasting decisions demand confirmation.

✧

Counterplay over grind

Distinct threats, readable tells, and opportunities to study or spare some guardians create routes beyond brute strength.

⋈

Living communities

Supplies, labor, testimony and care have local costs. A rescued crossing or reconciled household can shape a return visit.

◈

Consequences that persist

Confirmed world changes and remembered encounters are designed to survive saves without requiring completion of optional stories.

These describe authored rules and source-level systems, not claims that the full game is built or verified in Unreal Engine.
