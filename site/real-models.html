02 / SOURCE-AUTHORED PLAYER EXPERIENCE

What a real visit
should mean.

Bellwold's Afterlight quest provides a specific example of the RPG's intended exploration, evidence, decisions and persistent aftermath. This is a source-backed design sequence, not a verified complete Unreal playthrough.

01 / EXPLORE

An absent bell, an occupied refuge.

Reach Bellwold's gray-stone shelter under a copper-green tower with an empty bell yoke. Lanterns glow outside the refuge. Hessa and her neighbors are occupied with real community needs. Yet people remember hearing a bell that was never made.

Walk, observe, talk and investigate instead of simply following a fantasy quest marker.

02 / FIND ACTUAL EVIDENCE

Records against immediate needs.

After completing the earlier lantern and compact story, investigate either the western relief cache or the southern altered census. The cache has evidence of families needing shelter tonight. The altered census documents officially erased names. Healer Sorin or tutor Ivera must personally verify the matching find.

Hearsay cannot automatically substitute for first-hand proof.

03 / CHOOSE IN HESSA'S PRESENCE

Save lives tonight, or preserve names forever?

With proper evidence and witnesses, open a Safe Ward to shelter unregistered families immediately or commission an Open Census to preserve the erased public names. Neither choice magically resolves the other's problem. Each yields a unique one-time epic item.

Neither branch is labelled a simplistic good-versus-evil decision.

04 / RETURN

The world remembers what actually happened.

When you return, the visible landmark differs, eyewitness testimony changes, other residents have limited rumor knowledge, and the chosen outcome persists in saved world state. The refuge is still a place with unresolved needs, not a completed checkbox.

The core fantasy is an observable choice → witness → world change loop.

Combat, learning and nine non-linear realms

The source describes readable enemy tells, guard/dodge/line-of-sight counterplay, and skills learned through authored observation → understanding → experimentation → mastery. Watching a monster does not automatically unlock its powers. Player relationships, crafting, investigations and multiple routes are supposed to matter as much as combat.

What isn't verified: Actual Unreal rendering, navigation, rigged combat animation, sound, controller UI, collision and end-to-end saves/playthroughs. We won't claim a playable release until those checks happen.
